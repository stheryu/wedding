The Wedding

Joohyun and Fin are getting married on Saturday, April 24, 2027 at Ilhwadang, Samcheonggak, a historic garden venue nestled in the mountains of Seoul, Korea. The ceremony begins at 4:00 PM, followed by dinner. The celebration continues at an after party that evening, and with a farewell brunch the next morning. The dress code is cocktail attire. If you can, we kindly ask that you avoid wearing black.

Travel

By Air

Incheon International Airport (ICN) is the main gateway. The express airport train (AREX) reaches Seoul Station in about 45 minutes.

Visa Info

Many nationalities can visit South Korea without a visa for up to 90 days. If you travel without a visa, you may also need a K-ETA (Korea Electronic Travel Authorization), applied for online before you fly. Many countries are exempt from the K-ETA only through December 31, 2026, so please check your country's requirements well in advance on the official K-ETA site.

Seoul

Jongno-gu

History lives here, quietly. Palaces, shrines, and alleyways that are centuries old, alongside buzzy cafés and independent restaurants.

Bukchon & Samcheong-dong

A short drive down the hill from the venue. Traditional hanok alleyways, charming tea houses, and the best independent cafés in Seoul.

Insadong

Tea houses, craft shops, and street food in narrow alleyways. A great place to pick up something to bring home.

Euljiro

Seoul's best kept secret. An old printing district turned into the city's most exciting bar and restaurant scene.

Hongdae

Seoul's university district. Street performances, indie music, cheap eats, and nightlife that runs late.

Itaewon

Seoul's most international neighborhood. Diverse restaurants, vintage shops, and nightlife until morning.

Hannam-dong

Boutique hotels, bookshops, and a café on every corner. Relaxed, walkable, and endlessly browsable.

Seongsu-dong

A former industrial district turned creative hub. Converted factories, emerging designers, specialty coffee.

Cheongdam-dong

Seoul's luxury flagship district. Designer boutiques, concept stores, and some of the city's finest restaurants.

Apgujeong

The original luxury district, home to the Galleria department store, Apgujeong Rodeo Street, and plenty of Korean celebrity culture.

Gangnam-gu

The neighborhood that gave the world its most famous earworm. Luxury brands and some of the best restaurants in the city.

Questions

Dress Code
Cocktail attire. Suits and cocktail dresses are perfect. If you can, we kindly ask that you avoid wearing black.
Do you have a registry?
Your presence is the greatest gift, and we know many of you are traveling a long way to be with us. We don’t have a registry, but for those who wish to give, there will be a station at the venue where you may leave a cash gift.
Is the venue indoors or outdoors?
The ceremony takes place in the gardens of Samcheonggak, with the reception moving indoors. We recommend a light layer for the evening.
Can I bring a guest?
Yes, please include them when you RSVP.
Are children welcome?
We love your little ones! The celebration is planned mostly with adults in mind, but if you’d like to bring your children, please include them in your RSVP so we can plan a seat and meal for each.
Is there parking at the venue?
Yes, parking is free at the ceremony venue.
When should I RSVP by?
We'd love to know by February 24, 2027.
